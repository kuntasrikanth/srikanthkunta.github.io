How I work with data

The habits, not the tools

Validate before you model. I build assertion tests so a fault surfaces automatically rather than producing a plausible wrong answer.

Stress-test your own preference. I ran the location model across every weighting scenario, not only the one I liked.

Score on what matters. Recall over accuracy when the rare failures are the point.

Document the limitations. Openly, in the report, where a reader can weigh them.

What three years actually taught me

I learned hooks by losing audiences

I check retention after every upload to find the exact second attention breaks. It is almost always the opening, and almost always because I led with context instead of the interesting part.

That is the only way the lesson holds. You can read about hooks for a year and not internalise it; watch your own graph fall off a cliff at four seconds and you never make that mistake again.
